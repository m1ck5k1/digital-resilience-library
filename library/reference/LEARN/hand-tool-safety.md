# Skills & Trades — Hand-Tool Safety

> Category: **LEARN** · Part: **Reference** (public)
> Primary source: **OSHA 3080** — *Hand and Power Tools* (US OSHA, public-domain).
> Verified 2026-09-08.
> ⚠ **General safety knowledge, not a substitute for the tool's manual or your local
> regulations.** This is the universal safety baseline, not tool-specific technique.

*"Hand tools are tools that are powered manually — anything from axes to wrenches. The greatest
hazards posed by hand tools result from misuse and improper maintenance."*

---

## 1. Hand tools — the hazards are misuse & neglect

| Hazard (if misused / badly maintained) | What can happen |
|----------------------------------------|-----------------|
| Chisel used as a screwdriver | The tip may break and fly off, hitting you or others. |
| Loose / splintered / cracked wooden handle (hammer, axe) | The head may fly off and strike you or others. |
| Sprung wrench jaws | The wrench can slip. |
| **Mushroomed** heads on impact tools (chisels, wedges, drift pins) | They can shatter on impact, sending sharp fragments flying. |
| Dull knifes / scissors | *"Dull tools can cause more hazards than sharp ones."* |

**Rule of thumb:** keep hand tools **sharp and properly maintained**; a cracked saw blade must be
**removed from service**; don't use a wrench with sprung jaws; keep impact tools free of
mushroomed heads; don't use a tool with a splintered wooden handle.

---

## 2. Sparks & flammable atmospheres

- **Iron or steel hand tools can produce sparks** — an ignition source around flammable
  substances. Where that hazard exists (e.g. near fuel/gas), use **spark-resistant tools made of
  non-ferrous materials**.

---

## 3. Personal protection & the workspace

- Wear appropriate **PPE** (e.g. safety goggles, gloves).
- Keep **floors clean and dry** to prevent slips.
- Secure the **work** with a clamp or vice so both hands are free.

---

## 4. Power tools — general precautions (from OSHA 3080)

- **Never** carry a tool by its cord/hose; **never yank** the cord/hose to disconnect.
- Keep cords/hoses away from **heat, oil, and sharp edges**.
- **Disconnect** tools when not in use, before servicing/cleaning, and when changing accessories.
- Keep uninvolved people at a **safe distance**.
- Avoid accidental starting — don't keep your finger on the switch while carrying a plugged-in tool.
- Maintain tools **sharp and clean**; follow the manual for lubrication / accessory changes.
- Keep good **footing and balance**; wear proper apparel — loose clothing, ties, and jewellery can
  catch in moving parts.
- **Remove and tag** damaged portable electric tools *"Do Not Use."*
