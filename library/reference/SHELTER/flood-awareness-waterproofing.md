# Shelter & Home — Flood Awareness & Basic Waterproofing

> Category: **SHELTER** · Part: **Reference** (public)
> Primary source: **Ready.gov — Floods** (US DHS/FEMA). Verified 2026-09-08 (Wayback replay).
> **Not a substitute for local instructions.** Flooding is the **most common disaster in the
> United States**; flash floods can come without warning.

---

## 1. Know the risk & prepare before

- **Know your flood risk** (FEMA Flood Map Service Center) — but *"flooding doesn't follow lines
  on a map. Where it rains, it can flood."*
- **Sign up for warnings** — community warning system, EAS, NOAA Weather Radio.
- **Flood insurance:** *"homeowner's insurance policies do not cover flooding."* Policies take up
  to **30 days** to take effect — buy well before a disaster (National Flood Insurance Program).
- **Make a plan** for your household **and pets** — where to go, what to protect.
- **Protect your property:**
  - **Move valuables to higher levels.**
  - **Declutter drains and gutters.**
  - **Install check valves.** *Consider a sump pump with a battery.*
  - Keep important documents in a **waterproof container** + create password-protected digital copies.

---

## 2. Under a flood warning / during a flood

- **Find safe shelter right away**; follow local instructions — evacuate if told to, move to
  higher ground/higher floor, or stay where you are.
- **Never walk, swim, or drive through flood water** — *Turn Around, Don't Drown.* **Six inches of
  moving water can knock you down; one foot can sweep a vehicle away.**
- **Stay off bridges over fast-moving water** — it can wash them away without warning.
- **Trapped in your car:** stay inside if it's in rapidly moving water; **get on the roof** if
  water is rising inside the car.
- **Trapped in a building:** get to the **highest level**; only get on the roof if necessary and
  **signal for help**. **Do not climb into a closed attic** (risk of being trapped by rising water).

---

## 3. After a flood

- **Return home only when authorities say it's safe.** Avoid driving except in emergencies.
- **Wear protective gear** — heavy work gloves, boots, long clothing; face coverings when cleaning
  mould/debris. People with asthma/lung conditions or immune suppression should not enter buildings
  with visible/smellable leaks or mould; children should not do cleanup.
- **Be aware of snakes/animals** that may have entered your home.
- **Electrocution risk:** *"Do not touch electrical equipment if it is wet or if you are standing
  in water."* Turn off the electricity if safe to do so.
- **Do not wade in floodwater** — it can be contaminated (sewage/chemicals/debris) and electrically
  charged by downed power lines.
- **Generators:** use any gasoline-powered machinery **only outdoors, away from windows** (CO hazard).
