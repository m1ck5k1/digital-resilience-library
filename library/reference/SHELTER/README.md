# SHELTER — Index

> Category: **SHELTER** · Emergency Shelter & Cold Survival · Reference.

Shelter is the layer that keeps you alive once you can't get home — and, in cold, it's the
difference between discomfort and hypothermia. Grounded in public-domain US institutional
sources: CDC / NIOSH (hypothermia & frostbite), NOAA / NWS (wind chill), the American Red
Cross, and US Army FM 21-76 (survival shelter doctrine).

## Why shelter sits here
WATER → FOOD → ENERGY → SAN → NAV → **SHELTER**. Navigation gets you to a safe place; shelter
with retained heat is what keeps you alive once you're there. SHELTER now covers both the
**improvised/cold** layer (survival shelter, hypothermia) and the **home/weather** layer (storm
protection, flooding, shelter-in-place) — the full shelter picture, all grounded in T1 sources.
The remaining categories (MED, LEGAL, SEC, COMMS, KIDS, LEARN) are covered elsewhere.

## Relationship to MED
This category is about **prevention and emergency survival** (recognising cold danger, keeping
warm, improvising shelter). Cold-injury **first aid** appears only as immediate emergency
reference from T1 institutional sources (CDC / Red Cross) and is **not a substitute for
professional medical care** — see the disclosure inside the cold-survival doc.
