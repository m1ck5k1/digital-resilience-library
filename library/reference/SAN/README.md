# SAN — Index

> Category: **SAN** · Sanitation & Hygiene · Reference.

Sanitation is the quiet killer in an emergency: where plumbing stops, unsafe excreta handling
and unwashed hands spread the diseases (diarrhoea, cholera, dysentery, typhoid) that actually
kill people during disasters — often more than the disaster itself. This category holds the
hygiene fundamentals.

## Reference docs (public)
- [`emergency-water-quantity.md`](./emergency-water-quantity.md) — how much water each person
  needs per day (1 gal/US FEMA; 15–20 L/day WHO breakdown), and how to make water safe for
  drinking + hand-washing (boil / bleach / tablets).
- [`hand-hygiene.md`](./hand-hygiene.md) — the five critical hand-washing moments, and soap
  vs **ash** as an acceptable substitute when soap is gone.
- [`excreta-disposal.md`](./excreta-disposal.md) — trench & pit latrine basics when plumbing
  is out, and the critical **well ≥15 m from sanitation** separation rule.

## Place in the build order
WATER → FOOD → ENERGY → **SAN** → (SHELTER, NAV…). Sanitation follows energy because, once the
power is managed, keeping waste and hands in check is what stops the aftermath from turning
into an outbreak.
