# Sanitation & Hygiene — Emergency Excreta Disposal (Trench & Pit Latrines)

> Category: **SAN** · Part: **Reference** (public)
> Primary source: **WHO** emergency-health guidance (disaster-management manual).
> Verified live 2026-09-07.
> **Not a substitute for professional engineering / WASH-agency guidance.** These are
> survival-context figures to apply with care, not building-code standards.

When plumbing is out — or for a long-term shelter/camp situation — safe excreta disposal is
the difference between a contained situation and a disease outbreak. Faeces from an ill
person carries the organisms that cause diarrhoea, cholera, dysentery, and typhoid.

---

## 1. Core principles

- **Everyone defecates in the designated place** — never in or near water sources, shelters,
  or food areas.
- **Cover faeces** after each use (breaks the fly + hand vector).
- **Keep excreta away from any water source** — separation is the single most important rule.

---

## 2. Planning a defecation / trench field

- Allow about **0.25 m² per person per day** (excluding access paths) — e.g. roughly
  **250 m² per 10,000 people per day**, i.e. about **2 hectares per week**.
- Site the field **not more than ~30 m** from shelters, on ground sloping **away** from the
  camp and from surface-water sources, in soil soft enough to dig.
- In a mixed community, provide **separate men's and women's areas**.

---

## 3. Latrine types (numeric guidance)

### Shallow trench latrine (quick, temporary)
- Trenches **20–30 cm wide, 15 cm deep**.
- Provide a shovel so users cover excreta; **cover with at least 10 cm of soil**.
- Open trenches one at a time; close/fill as they fill, then move on.

### Deep trench latrine (more capacity)
- **Max length 6 m** (≈ 6 cubicles), **0.8–0.9 m wide**, **top 0.5 m lined**.
- Cover with planks, leaving every 3rd/4th plank as the defecation slot.

### Raised-pit latrine (high water table / flood-prone ground)
- Pit bottom at least **1 m above the water level** (protects groundwater).
- Build the pit **1–1.5 m above ground level** where the water table is high.
- Line pits **without holes for 300 mm** each side of dividing walls.

### Offset / double-pit
- Space the pits at least **the depth of the pits apart** (prevents cross-contamination).

---

## 4. The critical separation: wells vs sanitation

- Locate a **drinking-water well at least 15 m from any sanitation facility and its
  discharge**. This distance separation is the guard against contaminating the water supply.
- Raised/covered pits, well-separated from wells, are how groundwater stays safe.

---

## 5. Hand-washing at the latrine

- Users should be able to **wash their hands with soap (or an alternative such as ash)**
  after defecating, and there should be a **source of water near the toilet** (see
  `hand-hygiene.md`).
- Provide culturally appropriate materials and disposal for anal cleaning and menstruation.
