# NAV — Index

> Category: **NAV** · Navigation & Survival · Reference.

Navigation is knowing where you are and getting where you need to be — and, critically,
what to do the moment you realise you're lost (before panic turns a walk into a survival
situation). Grounded in the US Army field manuals FM 3-25.26 (Map Reading & Land Navigation)
and FM 21-76 (Survival) — public-domain US government works.

## Reference docs (public)
- [`map-compass-lost.md`](./map-compass-lost.md) — azimuth/back-azimuth, declination, compass
  interference + accuracy, pace count & distance, and the **SURVIVAL** lost-procedure
  (Size-up → Understand → Remember → Vanquish fear → Improvise → Value living → Act → Learn).
- [`natural-navigation.md`](./natural-navigation.md) — finding direction with no compass:
  sun (shadow-tip, shadow-clock, watch method), moon, the North Star, the Southern Cross, and
  what NOT to trust (moss).
- [`emergency-signaling.md`](./emergency-signaling.md) — being seen and heard: signal fires +
  smoke, signal mirror, strobe/pen flares, whistle, ground-to-air code, SOS.
- [`dead-reckoning-pacing.md`](./dead-reckoning-pacing.md) — distance + route technique: your
  personal pace count, distance/time estimation, steering marks, detours, and the deliberate 10°
  offset. FM 3-25.26.

## Why navigation sits late in the build order
WATER → FOOD → ENERGY → SAN → **NAV** → SHELTER. For a household in a disaster, navigation
matters once you must *move* — evacuating, finding a safe ground, or being rescued. It's the
skill that turns "lost" into "found again". SHELTER follows it because shelter-with-heat is
the next survival layer when you can't get home.

## Status note on this seed
