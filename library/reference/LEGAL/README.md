# LEGAL — Index

> Category: **LEGAL** · Legal & Documentation · **Template only.**

Legal/documentation content is entirely about a household's own documents and records. There
is **no universal reference** here — the value is a household's private inventory of where its
important papers live. That inventory is **household data**, so it stays **with the household**
(never in this repo).

## Household template (`library/templates/`)
- [`LEGAL-important-papers-inventory.md`](../templates/LEGAL-important-papers-inventory.md)
  — a blank scaffold to map where your identity/vital records, property, financial accounts,
  wills, and emergency documents are stored, and who can access them. Fill locally; the filled
  form is your private record.

## Why LEGAL is template-only
A legal "reference" would either be generic legal advice (lipability heavy, not the library's
role) or household-specific documents (identifying, stays with you). The safe, useful public
artifact here is the **blank scaffold** — the *form*, not any legal content or any household
data. Per `AGENTS.md`, filled legal inventory lives under the user's own 3-2-1 cold/private
storage, never here.
