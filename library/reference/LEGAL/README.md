# LEGAL — Index

> Category: **LEGAL** · Legal & Documentation · **Reference (generic) + Template.**

Legal content here has two very different halves:

- **Generic reference** — preparedness/records *organization* knowledge usable by anyone,
  deliberately free of legal advice and of any personal data.
- **Household data** — the actual inventory of *your* documents and who can access them.
  That is **household data**, so it stays **with you** (never in this repo).

## Reference docs (public)
- [`important-papers-records.md`](./important-papers-records.md) — what a records kit is
  (identity / financial / health buckets), where to keep it (3-2-1, fire/waterproof), what to
  grab when evacuating, and how to refresh it. Grounded in FEMA **Emergency Financial First
  Aid Kit (EFFAK)** + Ready.gov. **Not legal advice.**

## Household template (`library/templates/`)
- [`LEGAL-important-papers-inventory.md`](../templates/LEGAL-important-papers-inventory.md)
  — a blank scaffold to map where your identity/vital records, property, financial accounts,
  wills, and emergency documents are stored, and who can access them. Fill locally; the filled
  form is your private record.

## Scope (operator decision 2026-09-18)
Previously "template only." On operator approval, one **generic, non-advice** reference
(preparedness/records organization, from federal sources) was added to give the category
usable public depth. The **identifying inventory remains template-only** — a legal "reference"
that carried household documents or jurisdiction-specific legal claims is out of scope.
Per `AGENTS.md`, filled legal inventory lives under the user's own 3-2-1 cold/private storage,
never here.
