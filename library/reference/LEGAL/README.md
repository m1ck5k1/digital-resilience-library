# LEGAL — Index

> Category: **LEGAL** · Legal & Documentation · **Reference (framework + records) + Template.**

Legal content has two halves:

- **Generic reference** — the forms *framework* + a practical records-kit *organization* guide,
  usable by anyone, deliberately free of legal advice and of any personal data.
- **Household data** — the actual inventory of *your* documents and who can access them.
  That is **household data**, so it stays **with you** (never in this repo).

## Reference docs (public)
- [`legal-reference-index.md`](./legal-reference-index.md) — essential documents & forms
  *framework* (what a will / durable POA / health directive covers, official-form sources,
  document-inventory categories) **plus** a practical **records kit** section (identity /
  financial / health buckets, 3-2-1 storage, what to grab when evacuating, refresh cadence).
  Grounded in FEMA **Emergency Financial First Aid Kit (EFFAK)** + Ready.gov + official US
  forms. **Not legal advice.**

## Household template (`library/templates/`)
- [`LEGAL-important-papers-inventory.md`](../templates/LEGAL-important-papers-inventory.md)
  — a blank scaffold to map where your identity/vital records, property, financial accounts,
  wills, and emergency documents are stored, and who can access them. Fill locally; the filled
  form is your private record.

## Scope & provenance (operator decision 2026-09-18)
The generic LEGAL reference was first added by an autonomous lane on **2026-09-13** and
consolidated (records-kit section merged in) on **2026-09-18** per operator scope approval ("A:
add generic legal reference pages"). The **identifying inventory remains template-only** — a
legal "reference" that carried household documents or jurisdiction-specific legal claims is
out of scope. Per `AGENTS.md`, filled legal inventory lives under the user's own 3-2-1
cold/private storage, never here.
