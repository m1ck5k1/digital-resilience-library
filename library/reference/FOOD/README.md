# FOOD — Index

> Category: **FOOD** · Reference + one Template.

This is the second seeded category (after WATER). Chain of priority: water first, then
**food** — once hydration is secured, sustained survival depends on calories, nutrition, and
storable food.

## Reference docs (public)
- [`storage.md`](./storage.md) — building a food pantry / emergency supply: what stores well,
  conditions, rotation, and the well-attested rules of thumb (dry-storage temp, danger-zone).
- [`preservation.md`](./preservation.md) — methods that extend food without power: drying,
  canning, fermenting, root-cellar, salting/sugaring. General method guidance.
- [`sources-and-safe-hold.md`](./sources-and-safe-hold.md) — where known precise shelf-life
  and canning-process numbers are REQUIRED to be confirmed (flagged for the review gate).

## Household template
- [`FOOD-pantry-plan.md`](../templates/FOOD-pantry-plan.md) — fill in YOUR household's pantry
  stock, seed store, and preservation plans. Fill locally; your answers never go in this repo.

## Place in the build order
WATER → FOOD was the agreed seed order (body hydration, then calories/nutrition). MED,
ENERGY+SHELTER, SAN, NAV follow in later passes.

## Status note on this seed
`storage.md` and `preservation.md` are written from **well-attested, public-domain general
guidance**, kept intentionally general rather than quoting precise per-food shelf-life tables
that could not be verified against a live source during this pass. Where an exact number is
load-bearing, it is routed to `sources-and-safe-hold.md` as a **review/confirm item** for the
human gate (the 20%). None of this changes the fact-level method guidance.
