# MED — Household Emergency Health Plan (Template)

> **What this is:** a blank scaffold to capture YOUR household's emergency health plan. Fill it

> never uploaded and never enter the public library repo.** Back up your filled file 3-2-1.

> **Fill in the blanks.** Leave anything you don't yet know marked TODO and complete it.
