# LEGAL — Important-Papers & Documents Inventory (Template)

> **What this is:** a blank scaffold to record *where* your household's important documents
> live and who can access them. Fill it in locally. **These are your data — they are never
> uploaded and never enter the public library repo.** Back up your filled file 3-2-1.
>
> **Fill in the blanks.** Leave anything you don't yet know marked TODO and complete it.
> ⚠ The *detail* (account numbers, locations) is identifying — this is a **pointer/map**, not a
> vault. Do not put actual secrets in any file that leaves your device.
