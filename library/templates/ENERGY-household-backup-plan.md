## 3. Carbon-monoxide safety checklist (non-negotiable)
- [ ] Generator/engine **never** indoors, in garage, basement, crawlspace, shed, porch, deck, or balcony.
- [ ] Operated outdoors **≥ 20 ft** from windows, doors, and attached garages.
- [ ] CO alarms on **every level** (incl. basement), **≥ 15 ft** from fuel-burning appliances, near sleeping areas.
- [ ] CO alarms are **battery-operated or battery-backup** + tested **monthly**.
- [ ] Fuel stored **outdoors**, away from the home.
- [ ] Generator **cool before refuelling**; heavy-duty cords; dry/wet-hand rule.

## 5. Appliance & surge protection
- [ ] Freezer/fridge kept **closed** during outage (fridge ~4 hr, full freezer ~48 hr).
- [ ] Disconnect/trip heavy loads before power returns (surge risk).
- [ ] Thermometer on fridge/freezer to catch the **40°F discard** threshold.
